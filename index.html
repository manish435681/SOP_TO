1.3 · Applicability

This SOP applies to…

Who

All Train Operators / Emergency Train Operators authorized for performing train operation duty.

When and where

It is applicable to all time and places where Train Operator (TO) / Emergency Train Operator (ETO) is detailed to perform train operation.

1.4 · General

Technology does not replace the operator

Although the automatic train protection (ATP) system is available for safety in train operation, yet full attention, vigil, concern, dependability and judgment of TO / ETO is a pre-requisite, whenever entrusted with the responsibility of operating the trains.

1.5 · Definitions

Metro Railway General Rules 2020

“Train Operator”means the driver of the train responsible for movement and control of the train.
“Emergency Train Operator (ETO)”means an authorized person having valid certificate of competency for train operation, nominated by Traffic Controller to drive the train in an emergency.

Source: SOP Chapter 1, clause 1.5

Appearance, attitude and reporting

  1. Wear badge and proper uniform; stay neat and tidy. You are NMRC’s face to the public.

  3. Stay committed to the organization’s Vision, Mission and Culture.

  6. Keep Crew Control informed of your current address and contact number; update the Bio-data register.
  7. Report for duty at the time and place in your schedule, and at any other time or place required.

2.5 · First aid kit

Collect at Sign-ON. Return after the trip.

Collect the kit with other belongings at Sign-ON. It holds medicines and items approved by the Competent Authority. Items expire, so follow this procedure.

STEP 1Collect from DC/CC/SC before the trip or taking charge from TO.
STEP 2Return to DC/CC/SC after the trip or on handing over to another TO.
STEP 3Record any item used and inform DC/CC/SC.
STEP 4DC/CC/SC verify all items are within validity.
STEP 5CC/DC/SC monitor expiry and inform CDI in advance; CDI arranges replacement so the kit stays complete and serviceable.
STEP 6CC/DC/SC remove expired items at once and replace them.
Under no circumstances, no expired medicine or item may remain in the kit.

3.1 · Essential rest before duty

Duty needs full attention and judgment. Body and mind must be actively present, so take full rest for the advised period before reporting.

Full attentionJudgmentFull rest
3.2 · Attendance for duty

Attend duty as per the roster, and at any other time or place when instructed by the competent authority.

3.3 · Sign on procedure

At Crew Control / DCC / Station Control Room, TO/ETO shall:

  1. Undergo Breath Analyzer Test.
  2. Handle mobile / electronic gadget as per extant procedure.
  3. Read notices, especially those needing attention for the day and line.
  4. Sign ON in the Sign ON/OFF register.
  5. Collect train belongings: Mascon Key, Square Key, Radio Handset (Tetra), PSL Key, Train logbook (as applicable), First Aid Box and other equipment.
NOTE: ETO taking emergency charge where no sign-on/off facility exists gives BA self-declaration to station staff before boarding, and coordinates with Crew Control for a BA test after de-boarding, at the nearest facility.
3.4 · Sign-off procedure

At Crew Control / DCC / Station Control Room, TO/ETO shall:

  1. Report as per roster, or after stabling train at the designated location.
  2. Deposit train belongings (Radio Handset, Square Key, Mascon Key, Train Log Book, PSL Key, First Aid Box, other equipment) when stabling or finishing stepping back / double ending.
  3. Hand over Radio Handset and take receiving in Memo Book.
  4. Undergo Breath Analyzer Test.
  5. Handle mobile / electronic gadget as per extant procedure.
  6. Sign-OFF in the Sign-ON/OFF register.
  7. Confirm succeeding duty from roster / Crew Controller / concerned official.

Chapter 4

Taking Over & Handing Over Charge of Train

  1. 4.1Taking over charge: depot, main line, step back / double ending, in-person relief
  2. 4.2Handing over charge: depot, arriving TO/ETO, stabling, out-stationed trains, radio handset
4.1 (1) · In depot · a – f

Taking charge in depot

  1. After Sign-ON, collect all equipment and verify the train has a valid, signed fitness certificate.
  2. Confirm stabling location of the train with Depot Controller.
  3. Move for pre-departure checks (Preparation).
  4. Follow depot movement guidelines for personal safety.
  5. At the location, verify train set, configuration and location.
  6. Board the train as per extant procedure.
4.1 (1) · In depot · g – j

Taking charge: checks and readiness

  1. At the time of taking charge:
    1. Maintenance or housekeeping going on: do not take charge. Inform Depot Controller and seek instruction. After staff de-board, inform DCC the time and proceed to prepare.
    2. Work in the under-frame: inform Depot Controller and seek instruction. If the same train is offered, ask for fresh fitness.
  2. Conduct pre-departure checks and fill the train logbook.
  3. Defect affecting safety or comfort: report to Depot Controller on train radio at once, note in logbook, and report to OCC from the stabling location.
  4. Report readiness to Depot Controller; move after getting movement authorization.
4.1 (2) a · Outstation stabled train (overnight) · i – v

Fitness, location and boarding

  1. After Sign-ON, collect equipment and verify valid signed fitness. If valid, write on the first fresh logbook page: “Fitness is valid up to <date> & <time> and train consist is of 4/6-Car, endorsed on page no.___”. Fitness absent or invalid: do not take charge; inform OCC and seek instructions.
  2. Go to the designated location as per Day’s Roster or Crew Controller.
  3. Follow out-station movement guidelines for personal safety.
  4. At the location, verify train set, configuration and location.
  5. Board the train as per extant procedure.
4.1 (2) a · Outstation stabled train (overnight) · vi – ix

Preparation, defects and departure

  1. Conduct pre-departure checks and fill the logbook. Walk through the whole train; report improper cleanliness to TC/OCC.
  2. Defect affecting safety or comfort: report to OCC on train radio at once, seek instruction, note in logbook.
  3. Report readiness to OCC; move as per timetable and proceed signal, or as instructed by Traffic Controller.
  4. Degraded mode movement: move only after authorization from Traffic Controller.
4.1 (2) b · Stabled train (during revenue hours) · i – iv

Stabled train in revenue hours

  1. Take items from Crew Controller or Station Controller room; go to the stabled train as per roster or Crew Controller.
  2. Follow out-station movement guidelines for personal safety.
  3. Train stabled in open section, at induction: open saloon doors on both sides (non-passenger area) for one minute before starting AC, to vent stale air. First ensure no passenger inside and train not in platform area.
  4. Report readiness to OCC; move as per timetable and proceed signal, or as instructed. Degraded mode: only after Traffic Controller authorization.
4.1 (2) c · Relieving TO/ETO during step back / double ending · i – vi

Reaching, boarding and verifying

  1. Collect items from Crew Controller/Station Controller; reach arrival location of the train.
  2. Be at the location at least one minute before scheduled arrival.
  3. Check Train No. displayed at the front: correct train assigned to you.
  4. Board the designated cab.
  5. Verify all safety switches. Any not normal: inform OCC and seek instruction before moving.
  6. Verify train radio and hand portable set channels are in designated mode, at audible volume.
4.1 (2) d · Relieving TO/ETO in-person · i – iii

Position, identify and exchange information

  1. Be at the designated location at least one (1) minute before scheduled arrival.
  2. Check Train No. displayed at the front: correct train assigned to you.
  3. Arriving and Relieving TOs/ETOs exchange information about:
    1. Train status (driving mode).
    2. Train configuration.
    3. Any continuing fault.
    4. Any caution order.
    5. Status of safety switches.
    6. Any Person with Disabilities or DIVYANGJAN passenger, and de-boarding station.
    7. Any other important message.
4.1 (2) d · Relieving TO/ETO in-person · iv – ix

After taking charge and cab change

  1. Verify all safety switches. Any not normal and not told by arriving TO: inform OCC and seek instruction before moving.
  2. Verify radio and hand portable set channels and volume.
  3. Perform part of platform duty: ensure saloon doors open, and close doors before moving, observing boarding / de-boarding.
  4. Continued fault reported by Arriving TO: report it again to OCC.
  5. Before cab change / de-boarding at terminal or siding: put safety switches (except DBPS) back to normal. DBPS normalized only after OCC authorization.
  6. Carry belongings; all doors including cab doors closed and locked.
4.2 (1) · In depot

Handing over in depot

#Rule
aStable at the designated stabling point as per extant procedure.
bCarry belongings; all doors including cab doors closed and locked after de-boarding.
cDeposit train keys, Mascon key, square key, Train Log Book, Radio handset (Tetra), First Aid Box and PSL Key with Depot Controller.
dTrain in Aux-On handed directly to maintenance staff: only on Depot Controller authorization by radio.
4.2 (2) · Arriving TO/ETO during step back / double ending

Arriving TO/ETO

  1. After stopping at the designated point, opening saloon doors and PSDs (or ensuring they open) is the foremost duty.
  2. Before leaving the cab, handle safety switches as per extant procedure.
  3. Carry belongings; all cab doors including partition door closed and locked.
  4. Move to the other end / platform for the next train or roster activity.
  5. Reliever not present: do not leave the cab; inform crew control on radio and seek instructions. Never leave the train unmanned.
4.2 (3) · Arriving TO/ETO relieved in-person

Relieved in-person

  1. Foremost duty: open (or ensure opening of) saloon doors and PSDs at the designated stopping point.
  2. Leave the cab only when Relieving TO/ETO is available. If absent, inform crew control on radio and seek instructions. Never leave the cab unmanned.
  3. Exchange information with the reliever about:
    1. Train status (driving mode).
    2. Train configuration.
    3. Any continuing fault.
    4. Any caution order.
    5. Status of safety switches.
    6. Any Person with Disabilities or DIVYANGJAN passenger, and de-boarding station.
    7. Any other important message.
4.2 (4) · Stabling in main line (during revenue hours)

Stabling in main line

  1. All doors including cab doors closed and locked; no person or article left inside.
  2. Standby train in siding or platform (manned or unmanned): saloon AC switched off to save energy.
  3. Unmanning: ask Crew Controller/OCC whether to leave Aux-off or on. Aux-OFF from rear end cab only, then de-board.
  4. Deposit Mascon key, square key, Train Log Book, PSL Key, Radio handset (Tetra), First Aid Box as instructed.
4.2 (5) · Stabling as out-stationed (non-revenue hours)

Out-stationed trains

#Rule
aStable at the designated stabling point in main line as per extant procedure.
bAfter stabling in siding/platform, ensure all train doors are closed, walk inside to the rear end (open end) cab checking for abnormality, Aux-Off from the rear cab only, and de-board.
cCarry belongings; all doors including cab doors closed and locked.
dDeposit Mascon Key, square Key, Train Log Book, PSL Key, Radio handset with Crew Controller/Station Controller.
4.2 (6) · Portable radio handset (Tetra) · a – c

Issue and use

  1. Every train from depot or outstation, and TO/ETO starting stepping back, gets a Radio Handset as communication backup.
  2. Keep it OFF normally; switch ON when train radio fails or as required.
  3. On issue by DCC / Crew Control / Station Controller, give receiving in the Sign ON/OFF register.
4.2 (6) · Portable radio handset (Tetra) · d – f

Handover, placement and daily count

  1. On handing over to DC/SC/Crew Controller after stabling or end of step back / double ending, take receiving in your Memo book.
  2. Place keys and handset only at the designated place in crew control / SCR / DCC during break or sign off. At in-person crew change, Arriving TO hands over handset with other items; Relieving TO takes over.
  3. Night LS / Crew Controller counts handsets at crew control, outstations and DCC daily. Night count must equal morning issue. Mismatch: inform concerned CDI.

5.1 · General

Operate the train consistently and safely as per Rules & Procedures, Timetable and Traffic Controller instructions. Situation not covered and OCC not available: take the safest course of action.

5.2 · Respect to signals

Observe, repeat and act

  1. Obey line side signals along with cab signals, in ATP, ATO or any mode.
  2. Main line: repeat signal number, aspect and route indicator loudly before negotiating (e.g. Signal S126 B green with route).
  3. Depot: repeat signal number and aspect (e.g. Signal S141D is OFF) and en-route points and status (e.g. Point P129D is set in desired direction) to Depot Controller on train radio.
  4. Aspect changes during negotiation: repeat the status and act accordingly.
  5. Route indicator of permissive signal not as per plan: stop the train at once, inform OCC, seek instructions.
  6. Fixed signal blank but proceed code (Target speed) available: move as per extant procedure only.
  7. Proceed code available in front of a Danger signal: move as per extant procedure only.
5.3 · Vigil

Sharp lookout

01Stay vigilant and keep a sharp lookout on track, OHE and surroundings.
02Sun in your eyes: lower the sunblind to not more than 50% so visibility ahead is not affected.
03Avoid talking with the person in cab. If necessary, keep attention on the track.
04Be cautious entering platform area; blow horn if required for passenger safety.
05Crossing active neutral section: watch VCB open / close indications in cab. Abnormal: inform OCC.
5.4 · Dead man safety device (DSD)

Three rules for DSD

01 · CONTINUOUSOperate (twist) DSD continuously while moving in non-ATO mode.
02 · BYPASSDSD bypass switch must not stay bypassed in CBTC mode (ATP and ATO).
03 · RELEASERelease DSD only when stationary with TBC at Bmax.
5.5 · Stopping of train at station

Designated stopping point

#Rule
1Use the designated stop board for the stopping point at stations / sidings.
2Stops short, even in ATO: drive to the stopping point as per extant procedure before opening saloon doors.
3Stops beyond: ensure all doors are at platform and train is aligned before opening saloon doors and PSD (degraded movement). In ATO, doors open automatically; give only door close command and press ATO start.
4Stops beyond end of platform: inform OCC and seek instruction.
5In ATP/RM/ROS/FWD/CUTOUT modes keep TBC at Bmax at standstill.
5.6 · Platform duty

A. Saloon door opening

  1. At the stopping point, opening (or ensuring opening of) platform side saloon doors and PSDs is the foremost duty.
  2. Doors and PSDs open for due time; keep TBC at maximum braking while doors are open.
  3. Confirm all saloon doors and PSDs opened from indications. If not, act as per extant procedure.
  4. Observe boarding and alighting from available means.
  5. Train held beyond scheduled departure: keep doors and PSDs open unless Traffic Controller instructs otherwise.
5.6 · Platform duty

B. Saloon door closure

  1. Follow minimum dwell time (peak / non-peak) per timetable; improvise if dwell time is insufficient for the crowd.
  2. Play the complete door close announcement before the close command.
  3. As far as practicable, prevent doors striking passengers using rear view mirrors, train / platform CCTV.
  4. Before starting, confirm from indications that all doors (saloon, cab, PSDs) are closed with no infringement.
5.7 Departure · 5.8 ATO

Departure from station and ATO

5.7 (1)Depart on time as per timetable, unless Traffic Controller instructs otherwise.
5.7 (2)Train held beyond scheduled departure for any reason: inform OCC.
5.8 (1)Keep Master Controller / TBC at Bmax during ATO movement.
5.8 (2)Keep one hand on the emergency brake push button during ATO movement.
5.8 (3)Follow ATO/ATP driving timings strictly as per procedure.
5.9 Cab doors · 5.10 Speed

Cab doors and speed

5.9 (1)Keep cab doors and cab saloon partition door closed while the train moves.
5.9 (2)After stabling in depot / outstation, all cab doors closed and locked.
5.10 (1)Never exceed the Target or Advised speed.
5.10 (2)Depot speed: maximum 25 kmph or as advised on entry / exit.
5.11 · Use of timetable
  1. Read the day’s working timetable carefully: revenue service start / end time, rusty rail movement route and section, piloting section, minimum dwell time, and more. Follow it fully.
  2. No action causing deviation from schedule unless OCC instructs.
  3. OCC/TC changing the approved turn back plan informs TO/ETO with exchange of Private Numbers; TO/ETO confirms. Repeat every time a train is received on a platform other than the approved timetable.
NOTE: ETO may ask crew control or OCC about timetable queries.
5.12 Last revenue train · 5.13 Neutral section

5.12 So that no passenger for the advertised last revenue train to the terminal station is left at originating or interchange stations, TO/ETO of the last revenue train shall:

1Seek verbal confirmation for departure from the Station Controller of the starting station.
2Seek verbal confirmation for departure from the Traffic Controller before departing interchange stations.
5.13 · NEUTRAL SECTIONMonitor VCB opening and closing from cab indications. Abnormal behaviour: inform OCC and take necessary steps.
5.14 · Applying emergency brake
  1. Use only the designated emergency push-buttons or handles.
  2. Normalize the EB condition only after the train comes to a complete stop.
  3. EB not initiated by you: check the reason if possible and inform OCC at once. TC informs RSC / Signaling controller, who guide you as per procedure.
  4. Departing platform and PECU operated: apply EB immediately (passenger may face an emergency), try voice contact to learn the reason. No danger reported: inform TC/OCC, then depart.

7.1 · General

Safety has the utmost priority. Under no circumstances skip any step of a procedure having safety relevance.

7.2 (1) · Degraded mode guidelines · a – c

Reporting, authorization and limits

  1. Report any abnormality, failure or unusual circumstance promptly to OCC/DCC/SC as per train location.
  2. No degraded mode without OCC/DCC/SC authorization on exchange of private number. Exception: train stops before the NSP after entering platform: move to the NSP in degraded mode only after confirming the line of sight is clear. After docking and opening doors, inform OCC. Movement beyond the NSP needs Traffic Controller authorization first.
  3. Move only up to the authorized location. Further movement needs fresh authorization.
7.2 (1) · Degraded mode guidelines · d – g

Circumstances, discrepancies and signals

  1. Move in degraded mode in these circumstances only after OCC/SC authorization on private-number exchange:
    1. Loss of proceed code (Target Speed).
    2. Passing a signal at danger. Red / flickering / dual aspect signals are danger. Violet and blank are danger in degraded movement.
    3. ATP mode cannot be selected.
  2. Fixed signal shows no aspect but proceed code available: report the discrepancy to OCC/SC/DCC; move as per extant procedure.
  3. Proceed code available in front of Danger Signal: report the discrepancy; move as per extant procedure.
  4. Watch signals and points en-route, set in desired direction. Cross a signal only at the aspect authorized. If the aspect changes before crossing, stop at the foot of the signal and seek fresh instructions.
7.2 (2) · Steps for degraded mode · a

Requesting authorization

Signal at Danger and/or no proceed code: inform OCC/DCC/SC giving:

1Train number and exact location
2Signal number and aspect (if available)
3Status of track on line of sight, status of train and direction of travel

…and seek authorization to move in degraded mode.

7.2 (2) · Steps for degraded mode · b, c

Acknowledge, record and move

Repeat back: OCC/DCC/SC private no., train no., line of sight status, signal number and aspect, destination, and degraded mode authorized. Then give your private number and make the entry in the private number book.

  1. Passing a signal at danger on private number / written authority / Hand Signal: move cautiously, look out for points set for your direction, proceed with utmost vigilance, be ready to stop short of points not set properly.
  2. Under Hand Signal (Hands/Flags/Torch): announce on train radio / handset before moving, “Train-1 is getting Green <Lamp/Flag> hand signal and moving for <destination name>”.
  3. Follow 25 kmph, approved special instructions, or lower TSR/PSR speed.
  4. Be alert for any train or obstruction; be ready to stop short.
(c) After ATP mode resumes, inform OCC/SC/DCC.
7.3 · Vigilance during dense fog
  1. Dense fog affects visibility in open sections: be utmost vigilant.
  2. Active cab head lights on high beam.
  3. In ATO, stay ready to stop on any abnormality.
  4. In ATP/RM/ROS/FWD/CUTOUT, control speed to visibility, especially at platform approach.
  5. Blow horn entering or leaving platform.
  6. In degraded modes take special care about track clearance ahead.
  7. In degraded modes or depot with poor visibility: proceed cautiously, able to stop short of any obstruction.
7.4 · No mobile phones or electronic devices while driving

Mobile phones, musical instruments and other electronic devices divert attention. Using or not carrying them while driving is strictly prohibited.

Exception: TO may keep a phone or device in the Kit bag only in a sealed mobile pouch, switched off, when:
  1. Signing on in DCC/SCR/other Crew Control, for a continued trip from depot or outstation.
  2. On break / rest at a location other than the parent crew control.
  3. Signing off in DCC/SCR/other Crew Control, for a continued trip to depot or outstation.
  4. Pouch signed by CC/DC/SC on issue; opened only in their presence.
NOTE: ETO may keep a device switched off if pouch facility is unavailable at the boarding station.
7.5 Mobile pouch · 7.6 Horn in depot

7.5 Mobile pouch

  1. Make entry in Mobile Pouch Issue Register first.
  2. Show the switched-off device to on-duty CC/DC/SC; pouch is sealed, signed and stamped.
  3. Open the pouch only before on-duty CC/DC/SC, showing the device is switched off.
  4. Make entry in Mobile Pouch Open Register.

7.6 Horn in depot

  1. Blow horn before first movement from stabling location.
  2. Be vigilant at walkways; blow horn if required.
  3. Blow horn before moving again after a midway stop.
7.7 · Opening saloon doors when door authorization is not available

Door authorization protects platform side opening only. Without it: ensure the train is at the designated stopping point and/or all doors are at platform and not obstructed by PSDs. Open saloon doors and PSDs with OCC permission.

aOpen platform side cab door.
bPut one foot on the platform, enable PSL panel to confirm the platform, then follow extant procedure to open saloon doors / PSDs.
cClose cab door before departure.
7.8 · Closing saloon doors when system protection is bypassed

If protection is bypassed the train may run with doors open. Responsibility for moving with doors closed rests solely with the TO/ETO. Follow these steps with the extant door safety switch bypass procedure:

iOpen platform side cab door.
iiFoot on platform, give door close command, visually verify saloon doors / PSDs closed, and get Station Controller confirmation by radio before proceeding.
iiiClose cab door before departure.
7.9 · Other persons in cab of train

Cab travel is restricted so TO/ETO attention is not diverted. Staff other than notified TO/ETO/CDI/LS/Trainee carry a cab pass.

  1. Finish essential duties (opening saloon doors, PSDs) before opening the cab door / partition door.
  2. Check the cab pass and its validity, in mainline and depot.
  3. Inform OCC after the staff boards.
  4. No valid cab pass, no entry. Only in dire emergency, with OCC authorization.
  5. Know of any authorized staff boarding the rear cab.
  6. Normally not more than three persons including TO/ETO in the driving cab, unless authorized by superior / OCC.
7.10 Alerts · 7.11 Unusual occurrences

Alerts and unusual occurrences

7.10 · HIGH / RED / GENERAL ALERTBe utmost vigilant and cautious during movement. Report any unusual event or object to OCC immediately.
7.11 · UNUSUAL OCCURRENCESSafety is your first duty. Any unsafe condition or unusual occurrence: report to OCC immediately and seek instruction.
7.12 · No train enters depot / stabled / reversed through siding with any person without authority

Whenever operating a train:

  1. After opening saloon doors at the last revenue station, announce de-boarding of all passengers.
  2. Switch OFF all saloon AC if stabling in depot / siding.
  3. Reverse or stable the train only after clearance from deputed security staff / CFA that no unauthorized person is left in the train.
  4. Any abnormality: inform TC/OCC and Station Controller.
  5. Unscheduled withdrawal of train: OCC informs TO/ETO to act as per this procedure.

8.1 · General

Radio discipline

  1. Professional conversation only.
  2. Use only the dedicated channel / mode for OCC/SC/DCC/Crew Control.
  3. Identify yourself with Train No., Train set and location when starting.
  4. Confirm you are talking to the intended person, location and mode.

  6. Say “over” at the end of each message.
  7. Finish with “over and out”.
  8. No obscene or profane language; no unnecessary, irrelevant or distress communication.
  9. Do not let unauthorized persons use the communication system.

Chapter 9

Reversing of Trains in Main Line

  1. 9.1Reversal with one TO/ETO
  2. 9.2Reversal with two TO/ETO
9.1 (1) · Reversal from siding, stepping back not available

Overview: One TO/ETO drives the train to the applicable SRMB (Stopping Reverse Marker Board) in siding, de-activates the arrival end cab, changes cab, activates the departure end cab and drives from siding to platform following cab and fixed signals, or OCC instructions.

  1. Arriving or Relieving TO/ETO takes the train for reversal from siding at the terminal.
  2. Take the train to the designated stopping point (SRMB); de-activate the arrival end cab.
  3. Before leaving, close and lock cab doors / partition door; collect all train equipment.
  4. Change cab from inside the train.
  5. Walking through, look for abnormality, after ensuring cab-saloon partition doors are closed.
  6. Activate the departing end cab; depart as per extant procedure.
  7. Open saloon doors for boarding 30-45 sec before departure time at terminal.
9.1 (2) · Reversal at platform, stepping back NOT available

Overview: Same platform for arrival and departure, one TO/ETO. De-activate the arrival end cab, change cab, activate the departure end cab and drive.

  1. Stop at the designated stopping point; open platform side saloon doors / PSDs.
  2. De-activate the arrival end cab.
  3. Before leaving, close and lock cab side doors; collect all train operation equipment.
  4. Change cab, ensuring cab-saloon partition doors are closed. Walk to the departing end cab from outside the train, via platform.
  5. Activate the departing end cab; perform platform duty.
9.2 (1) · Stepping back operation (reversal at platform)

Overview: Same platform for arrival and departure, two TOs/ETOs. Arriving TO/ETO de-activates the arrival end cab and leaves. Relieving TO/ETO boards the departure end cab and drives. Arriving TO/ETO follows the roster.

  1. Arriving: stop at the designated point; open platform side saloon doors / PSDs.
  2. Arriving: de-activate the arrival end cab.
  3. Arriving: collect all equipment; close and lock cab doors and saloon partition door.
  4. Arriving: de-board and go to the departing end cab to check the reliever is there. If not, inform crew control on radio and seek instructions.
  5. Arriving: once the reliever is present, go to designated platform / location as per roster.
  6. Relieving: board the departure end cab.
  7. Relieving: activate the cab and perform platform duty.
9.2 (2) · Double-ending operation (reversal from siding) · Overview

Two TOs/ETOs reverse a train from siding. Relieving TO/ETO boards the rear cab at terminal platform. Arriving TO/ETO drives to the terminal siding and de-activates the arrival end cab. Relieving TO/ETO activates the departure end cab and drives from siding to platform. On arrival, Arriving TO/ETO de-boards.

Remember: Never leave the train unmanned. If the reliever is not present, inform crew control on radio and seek instructions.
9.2 (2) · Double-ending operation · Steps
  1. Arriving: stop at the designated point; open platform side saloon doors / PSDs.
  2. Relieving: board the rear end cab.
  3. Arriving: close doors after platform duty; take the train into siding up to SRMB; de-activate the arrival end cab.
  4. Arriving: close and lock cab doors including partition door before leaving the cab.
  5. Arriving: go inside the train to the departing end cab to check the reliever. If absent, inform crew control on radio and seek instructions.
  6. Relieving: activate the cab; depart as per extant procedure.
  7. Relieving: stop at the designated point; open platform side saloon doors / PSDs.
  8. Arriving: de-board and go to designated platform / location as per roster.
